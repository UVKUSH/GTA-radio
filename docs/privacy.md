# Privacy Policy — Wasted FM

_Last updated: 2026-07-24_

Wasted FM is a macOS app that plays YouTube videos, playlists, and podcasts
you choose, arranged as radio stations. This policy explains what happens
with your data.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Wasted FM has no analytics, no crash reporting, no telemetry,
and no account system. We don't operate any server that the app talks to,
and we never see your data.

## What's stored, and where

Your station layout ("wheels"), volume, and app settings are stored only in
this Mac's local application storage (`UserDefaults`), inside the app's
sandbox. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. If you share a wheel file with a
friend, that's a file you export and send yourself — we're not involved.

## Third-party content

Stations play content through YouTube's official embedded player. When you
play a station, your Mac connects directly to YouTube/Google's servers to
load that video or playlist — the same as opening YouTube in a browser.
That connection is governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy),
not by us. We don't proxy, log, or have access to this traffic.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes (e.g. a future feature adds real data
collection), this page will be updated and the update will be noted in the
app's release notes.
